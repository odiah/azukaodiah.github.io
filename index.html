Social & Personality Psychology · UT Austin

How do the spaces we live in shape how we feel?

I'm a Ph.D. candidate working with Sam Gosling. I study how architectural design — lighting, layout, color, materials — interacts with personality, emotion, and cognition to shape psychological experience. My tools span behavioral science, statistics, large language models, and generative AI.

Influenced by Wole Soyinka's philosophy of storytelling, I see spaces as narratives — places where design choices and sensory cues create meaning and guide how we live.

Outside of research, I swim, bike around Austin, collect African paintings, and sing with a jazz band.
